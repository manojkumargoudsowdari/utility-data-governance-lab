# Lab 14 — Enterprise Utility Platform Capstone

## Enterprise capabilities demonstrated

- End-to-end utility data platform architecture
- Source discovery and ingestion controls
- Curated dimensional modeling
- Data quality and reconciliation
- Lineage, ownership, and impact analysis
- Operational readiness and recovery

## Objective

Demonstrate the complete local utility data platform using evidence from Labs 01–13.

The goal is to show how scattered operational utility data can be discovered, ingested, modeled, validated, governed, and operated in a controlled local environment.

## Build

Create:

- `demo_runbook.md`
- `evidence_map.md`
- `architecture_diagram.md`
- `operational_controls.md`
- `platform_limitations.md`

## 30-minute practical demonstration

1. Show scattered source domains and their owners.
2. Run or show controlled raw ingestion.
3. Show standardized and curated models.
4. Demonstrate a quality gate and exception.
5. Trace one field through mapping and lineage.
6. Process one incremental AMI batch.
7. Show an RBAC denial and masked view.
8. Compare one query before and after tuning.
9. Explain one production-style incident and recovery.
10. Show backup/restore or warehouse recovery evidence.

## Evidence map

For each major platform capability, record:

- the lab that proves the capability;
- the exact artifact or command;
- the business problem being solved;
- the technical implementation;
- the validation or control used;
- one known limitation or production-scale improvement.

## Collaboration simulation

Write a short decision record for a disputed KPI or source definition.

Include:

- business owner
- architect
- source-system subject matter expert
- data steward
- security reviewer
- engineering owner
- vendor or external system owner, if applicable
- approver
- decision
- downstream impact

## Operational scenario evidence

Practiceare three evidence-based platform scenarios:

- Ownership: duplicate or failed incremental load
- Collaboration: grain, KPI, or source-definition alignment
- Operational excellence: quality gate, recovery, or performance improvement

## Completion criteria

- Every major platform capability maps to implementation evidence.
- The architecture can be explained from source to curated layer.
- Local simulation boundaries are documented clearly.
- The demonstration starts with business outcome, then technical design, validation, operations, and result.
- The demonstration can be completed from a clean runbook.
